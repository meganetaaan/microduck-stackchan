## Scope and modifications

This attribution applies to the retained MicroDuck leg/pelvis/battery and other inherited model content, and to derivative geometric content incorporated into the Tab5/Stack-chan prototype generations and arm concepts. It remains relevant after conversion, combining parts, mesh processing, MuJoCo compilation, compression, or rendering.

Project modifications include removal of the articulated head/neck, addition of fixed Tab5 body and enclosure variants, rounded and portrait printable concepts, UART/electronics/wiring proxies, arm-design concepts, collision decomposition, and browser visualization/compiled model packaging. Per-variant README files and generation/validation records describe the changes. These are independent experimental adaptations, not original manufacturer CAD or endorsed products.

When sharing original or modified geometry-bearing material, retain Pollen Robotics attribution, the source link, this license declaration, and a description of changes. Preserve the upstream noncommercial and share-alike conditions. Do not add a blanket Apache or MIT label to these assets. This file is a record of the upstream declaration, not a replacement license or a legal assurance.
